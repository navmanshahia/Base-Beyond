# Base & Beyond — Product Context

## Surface
Brand / marketing website for a premium digital studio.

## Audience
Canadian small businesses, service companies, founders and operators who need websites, portals, CRM-like systems, automation and ongoing web care.

## Primary goal
Make Base & Beyond feel materially more creative and capable than a generic website agency, while still making services, real work and pricing easy to understand.

## Proof
Real projects shown on the site:
- KBoom — cleaning service platform with quote and pricing workflows.
- TripFlow — travel operating system / PWA.
- SecureLink Tech — field operations, technician, owner and admin portal ecosystem.

## Commercial model
One-time website builds begin at CAD $599, with higher tiers at $999 and $1,499+, plus optional monthly care tiers.

## Experience principles
- Cinematic brand surface, not a dashboard.
- Distinctive composition without sacrificing comprehension.
- Motion should direct attention, explain continuity or acknowledge interaction.
- Mobile must feel intentionally composed, not like a collapsed desktop.
- Accessibility, reduced-motion support, keyboard focus and touch targets are non-negotiable.
